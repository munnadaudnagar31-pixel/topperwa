Ode on Solitude
Q1. How does the tree help the happy man?
Answer: According to Alexander Pope, the trees help the happy man by providing him with shade in the summer and wood for fire in the winter.
Me and the Ecology Bit
Q2. Which animal messes up Ms. Greene's yard?
Answer: It is the narrator's (Jim's) dog that digs up Ms. Greene's garden and messes up her yard.
Gillu
Q3. How did Gillu nurse the narrator during her indisposition?
Answer: When the narrator was ill, Gillu would sit near her head on her pillow and gently stroke her forehead and hair with his bushy tail, acting like a nurse.
Indian Culture
Q4. Which spirit underlies the changes of Indian history?
Answer: The spirit of "Unity in Diversity" underlies the changes of Indian history. Despite many invasions and foreign influences, Indian culture has maintained a unique continuity.
Polythene Bag
Q5. In the poem 'Polythene Bag', what causes pain?
Answer: The 'hurt' or pain caused by the polythene bag keeps coming back. It represents the lasting pain of separation or grief that, like plastic, does not dissolve or go away easily.
The Empty Heart
Q6. Why was the man given seven pots of gold?
Answer: The man was given seven pots of gold to satisfy his greed. However, instead of being happy, he became more greedy and wanted to fill the eighth pot, ruining his health and peace of mind.
Thinner Than a Crescent
Q7. Why is Radha crying?
Answer: Radha is crying because of the intense pain of separation from her beloved Lord Krishna. Her grief is so deep that she has become thinner than the crescent moon.
