About Me

I'm a passionate developer and educator from Bihar who built Topperwa to solve a real problem I saw around me — BSEB students struggling to find good, free, digital study resources in one place.

After personally experiencing the gap between what students need and what's available, I decided to build Topperwa — a free, modern, and powerful academic platform designed specifically for Bihar Board students.
