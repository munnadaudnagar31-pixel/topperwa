Physics • Optics
Q1. Why is a Concave Lens called a Diverging Lens?
Concept: A concave lens is thinner at the center and thicker at the edges.

Reason: When a parallel beam of light falls on it, the lens refracts the light rays outwards (away from the principal axis). Since the rays spread out or "diverge" after passing through, it is called a Diverging Lens.
Physics • Electricity
Q2. State Joule's Law of Heating.
Statement: The heat (H) produced in a resistor is:
1. Directly proportional to the square of current (I²).
2. Directly proportional to the resistance (R).
3. Directly proportional to the time (t) current flows.

Formula: H = I²Rt

Chemistry • Acids
Q5. Why does distilled water not conduct electricity, whereas rain water does?
Distilled Water: It is a pure covalent compound with no free ions. Hence, no conduction.

Rain Water: It dissolves gases like CO₂ and SO₂ from air to form carbonic/sulphuric acid. These acids dissociate into ions (H⁺), which conduct electricity.

Physics • Long • 5 Marks
Q10. Explain the Human Eye defects Myopia and Hypermetropia with causes and correction.
1. Myopia (Near-sightedness):
A person can see nearby objects clearly but distant objects appear blurry.
* Causes: Excessive curvature of eye lens (high power) or elongation of the eyeball.
* Image Formation: Image forms *in front* of the retina.
* Correction: By using a Concave Lens of suitable power.

2. Hypermetropia (Far-sightedness):
A person can see distant objects clearly but nearby objects appear blurry.
* Causes: Focal length of eye lens is too long or eyeball becomes too small.
* Image Formation: Image forms *behind* the retina.
* Correction: By using a Convex Lens.
Physics • Long • 5 Marks
Q11. Describe the principle and working of an Electric Motor.
Principle: An electric motor works on the principle that when a current-carrying coil is placed in a magnetic field, it experiences a force (Lorentz force) that rotates it.

Construction:
1. Armature: A rectangular coil (ABCD).
2. Field Magnet: Strong permanent magnet.
3. Split Ring (Commutator): Reverses current direction every half rotation.
4. Brushes: Carbon brushes pass current to the ring.

Working: Current flows ABCD. Force on arm AB is downward, CD is upward (Fleming’s Left Hand Rule). The coil rotates anti-clockwise. The commutator reverses current, maintaining continuous rotation.
Physics • Long • 5 Marks
Q12. Derive the expression for equivalent resistance in Series Combination.
Let three resistors R₁, R₂, R₃ be connected in series.
Key Concept: Current (I) is same, Voltage (V) divides.

Total Voltage V = V₁ + V₂ + V₃ ... (i)
According to Ohm's Law (V=IR):
V₁ = I R₁
V₂ = I R₂
V₃ = I R₃

Substituting in (i):
IR_eq = IR₁ + IR₂ + IR₃
Taking I common and cancelling:
R_eq = R₁ + R₂ + R₃

Q13. Explain the Dispersion of Light by a Glass Prism. What is a Spectrum?
Dispersion: The splitting of white light into its component colors (seven colors) when passing through a transparent medium like a prism is called dispersion.

Cause: Different colors have different speeds in glass. Red bends the least, Violet bends the most.

Spectrum: The band of seven colors obtained on a screen is called a Spectrum. The sequence is VIBGYOR (Violet, Indigo, Blue, Green, Yellow, Orange, Red).
Physics • Long • 5 Marks
Q14. State Ohm's Law and describe an experiment to verify it.
Ohm's Law: At constant temperature, the current (I) flowing through a conductor is directly proportional to the potential difference (V) across its ends.
V = IR

Experimental Verification:
1. Set up a circuit with a resistor, ammeter (series), voltmeter (parallel), battery, and rheostat.
2. Vary the current using the rheostat and note readings of I and V.
3. Plot a graph of V vs I.
4. Result: The graph is a straight line passing through the origin, proving V/I is constant (R).
Physics • Long • 5 Marks
Q15. What is a Solenoid? How does it behave like a magnet?
Solenoid: A coil of many circular turns of insulated copper wire wrapped closely in the shape of a cylinder.

Behavior:
* When current flows, magnetic field lines inside are parallel straight lines (uniform field).
* One end acts as the North Pole and the other as the South Pole.
* It behaves exactly like a bar magnet. The strength can be increased by inserting a soft iron core (Electromagnet).

Ray Diagram Logic:
1. Ray parallel to principal axis → passes through Focus (F).
2. Ray passing through Focus → becomes parallel to axis.
3. Intersection occurs exactly at C.

Nature of Image: Real, Inverted, and Same Size as the object.
Chemistry • Long • 5 Marks
Q17. Explain the cleansing action of Soap (Micelle Formation).
Structure of Soap: A soap molecule has two ends:
1. Hydrophilic Head: Ionic end (dissolves in water).
2. Hydrophobic Tail: Carbon chain (dissolves in oil/dirt).

Mechanism:
* When soap is in water, the molecules arrange in a cluster called a Micelle.
* The tails stick to the oily dirt (center), and heads face outward into the water.
* Agitation pulls the dirt out in the form of an emulsion, washing it away.

2. Making toys and statues.
3. Making surfaces smooth before painting.

Chemistry • Long • 5 Marks
Q20. What is an Homologous Series? Give characteristics.
Definition: A series of carbon compounds that have the same functional group and similar chemical properties.

Characteristics:
1. Successive members differ by -CH₂- group.
2. They differ in molecular mass by 14u.
3. They have similar chemical properties but graduating physical properties.
Example: Alkanes (Methane CH₄, Ethane C₂H₆).

Setup:
* Anode: Impure Copper block.
* Cathode: Thin strip of Pure Copper.
* Electrolyte: Copper Sulphate (CuSO₄) solution.

Process: On passing current, pure Cu from Anode dissolves into electrolyte and deposits on the Cathode. Impurities settle at the bottom as 'Anode Mud'.

Q25. Explain the process of Nutrition in Amoeba (Holozoic).
Amoeba is unicellular. Steps of nutrition:
1. Ingestion: It extends finger-like Pseudopodia to engulf food forming a Food Vacuole.
2. Digestion: Enzymes enter the vacuole and break down food.
3. Absorption: Digested food diffuses into the cytoplasm.
4. Assimilation: Used for energy and growth.
5. Egestion: Undigested residue is expelled by cell membrane rupture.

Q26. Describe the structure and function of a Nephron.
Nephron is the structural and functional filtration unit of the Kidney.

Structure:
* Bowman’s Capsule: Cup-shaped end containing Glomerulus (capillary bunch).
* Tubular Part: PCT, Henle’s Loop, DCT, Collecting Duct.

Function (Urine Formation):
1. Filtration: Blood is filtered in Bowman's capsule.
2. Reabsorption: Useful substances (glucose, water) are reabsorbed in the tubule.
3. Secretion: Wastes are added to forming urine.

Biology • Long • 5 Marks
Q28. Explain Double Circulation in Humans.
Blood passes through the heart twice in one complete cycle.

1. Pulmonary Circulation: Deoxygenated blood moves from Right Ventricle → Lungs → Left Atrium (gets Oxygenated).
2. Systemic Circulation: Oxygenated blood moves from Left Ventricle → Body Organs → Right Atrium (gets Deoxygenated).

Significance: It prevents mixing of oxygenated and deoxygenated blood, ensuring high energy supply.

Q30. Explain Mendel's Law of Dominance with a Monohybrid Cross.
Law: In a cross between two parents pure for contrasting traits, only one form of the trait (Dominant) appears in the F1 generation, while the other (Recessive) remains hidden.

Example (Tall vs Dwarf):
Parents: TT (Tall) x tt (Short)
F1 Generation: All Tt (Tall).
Here, Tallness is dominant over shortness.
Biology • Long • 5 Marks
Q31. How is the sex of a child determined in human beings?
Sex determination is genetic.
* Male: XY chromosomes. Produces two types of sperms (50% X, 50% Y).
* Female: XX chromosomes. Produces only X type eggs.

Process:
* If sperm carrying X fertilizes egg (X) → Girl (XX).
* If sperm carrying Y fertilizes egg (X) → Boy (XY).
Thus, the father determines the sex of the child.
Biology • Long • 5 Marks
Q32. What is a Reflex Arc? Trace the path.
Reflex Arc: The pathway taken by nerve impulses in a reflex action (sudden involuntary response).

Path:
1. Receptor: Detects stimulus (e.g., Heat).
2. Sensory Neuron: Carries signal to Spinal Cord.
3. Relay Neuron: Processes signal in Spinal Cord.
4. Motor Neuron: Carries signal to muscle.
5. Effector: Muscle contracts (Hand withdraws).
