❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is Topperwa free to use?

Yes! Topperwa is completely free for all Bihar Board students. We sustain our platform through advertisements.

How can I report an error in content?

You can email us at edubzzofficial@gmail.com or use the contact form above. Please mention the page name and specific error.

Is Topperwa affiliated with BSEB?

No. Topperwa is an independent educational platform. We are not affiliated with or endorsed by BSEB or any government body.

How quickly do you respond to messages?

We typically respond within 24–48 hours. For urgent issues, please reach out via Telegram for faster support.
