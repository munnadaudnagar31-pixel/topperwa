About Topperwa

The free, all-in-one BSEB study platform built for Bihar's students.

Our Mission

Topperwa was born with one goal: to democratize quality education for BSEB students across Bihar. We believe every student — regardless of their city, school, or financial background — deserves access to high-quality study material, smart tools, and proper exam guidance.

Topperwa provides chapter-wise MCQ quizzes, VVI question banks, model papers, an AI study assistant, and productivity tools — completely free.
